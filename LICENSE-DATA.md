## Per-entry caveat

CC BY 4.0 covers only Signal Lab's own compilation: the selection, structure, descriptions and notes. **Each listed data source keeps its own licence and terms**, which belong to its provider and can change. The `licence`, `licence_url` and `licence_note` columns record what we found when we reviewed the entry; they are information, not legal advice and not a grant of rights in the underlying data. Always check the provider's current terms before reusing data from a source, and follow any attribution it requires (see the `attribution` column).

Signal Lab is not affiliated with or endorsed by any provider; provider names are used only to identify them.
